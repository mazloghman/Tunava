Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2026

Tunava is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application.

Data Collection

Tunava does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics.

Microphone Access

Tunava requests access to your device's microphone solely for real-time pitch detection. Audio is processed entirely within your browser using the Web Audio API. No audio data is ever recorded, stored, or sent to any server.

Local Storage

Tunava uses your browser's local storage only to remember your settings and theme preference. No other data is stored.
