Tunava

The name Tunava blends "Tune" with "Navā" (نوا) — the Persian word for melody and sound, a term deeply rooted in classical Persian music theory. It represents the harmony between precision tuning and musical tradition.

Tunava is a professional-grade chromatic instrument tuner that runs entirely in your browser. No installation, no ads, no data collection — just open and play.

How it works: Tunava uses advanced pitch detection (YIN + Harmonic Product Spectrum) with Kalman-inspired adaptive smoothing to analyse your microphone input in real time.

Features:

  • Chromatic tuning with instrument presets (Guitar, Bass, Violin, Ukulele)
  • Detects frequencies from 20 Hz to 5000 Hz — bass to piccolo
  • Three notation systems: Letter (C D E), Solfege (Do Re Mi), and Persian with quarter-tone support
  • Five temperaments: Equal, Pythagorean, ¼-Meantone, Werkmeister III, Vallotti
  • Strobe and pitch-graph visualizations
  • Confidence gating and adaptive noise floor
  • Adjustable A4 reference pitch (392–466 Hz)
  • Dark and light themes
  • Transposing instrument support (B♭, A, F, E♭)

Tips for best results:

  • Use in a quiet environment for the most stable readings
  • Play single notes — chords will confuse the detector
  • Allow a moment after starting for noise floor calibration
  • Increase the buffer size (16K/32K) for better low-frequency accuracy

Built with the Web Audio API. All processing happens locally in your browser — no audio is ever transmitted.
